Recommended first field target
5–12kbit/s

Start with QPSK rate‑1/2 and adaptive band selection. Move to rate‑2/3 or 8‑PSK only after measured per-carrier SNR and packet error rate justify it.

Absorption

Thorp estimate: 8.28 dB/km at 30 kHz. Across 20–40 kHz, absorption changes by about 8.8 dB/km.

Doppler

1 m/s radial motion shifts a 30 kHz carrier by roughly 20 Hz. Wideband resampling should precede pilot correction.

Multipath

Harbor measurements have shown tens of milliseconds of delay spread—well beyond a 1.024 ms CP.

If the real transducer chain supports only 10 kHz of useful bandwidth, expect roughly half these rates.

Research context: Socheleau et al., 2012 (real-channel OFDM capacity); Ebihara et al., 2018 (mobile harbor delay and Doppler); MinhHai et al., 2016 (7.5 kbit/s, 8 kHz band, moving 30 m link). Final rates require tank and field channel sounding.
